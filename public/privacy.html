Privacy Policy

Last updated 11 November 2026

  • Your settings, tabs, history and site logins stay in your browser. We don't keep them.
  • We don't log the pages you visit.
  • Ads load only if you accept, and you can change that in Settings.
  • Sites in Atlas share one address, so avoid signing in to important accounts.

Atlas is a web browser that runs inside your own browser. This page explains what Atlas keeps, what passes through our servers, and which other services are involved.

What stays on your device

Your settings, shortcuts, open tabs, browsing history, and the cookies and saved data of sites you visit are stored in your browser on your device. We do not receive or back them up. You can delete them at any time from a site's Site Data panel in Atlas or by clearing your browser's data for this site.

What passes through our servers

  • Pages you open are fetched through our relay. With the Scramjet and Ultraviolet engines, secure (HTTPS) pages are encrypted between your browser and the site, so our servers cannot read their contents. If Atlas falls back to its compatibility engine, pages are processed on our servers to be displayed.
  • Search suggestions send what you type in the search box to our server, which asks Google for suggestions.
  • We use your IP address briefly in memory to apply rate limits that protect the service. Atlas does not keep logs of the pages you visit or the requests you make.

Other services

  • Advertising. Atlas shows ads from a third-party ad network on its home and settings pages. Ads only load after you accept on the ad banner. The ad network may use cookies or similar technology to show and measure ads, under its own privacy policy. You can change your choice at any time in Settings, Privacy, Ad Cookies.
  • Cloudflare. Our sites are delivered through Cloudflare, which processes connection data such as your IP address to deliver and protect them and may collect anonymous usage statistics.
  • Google Fonts. Atlas loads its font from Google, which receives your IP address when the font is downloaded.
  • Sites you visit. Websites you open in Atlas have their own privacy policies and may use cookies within Atlas.

Signing in to accounts

Every site you open in Atlas runs on the same Atlas web address. Atlas keeps each site's data apart in normal use, but a site built to do so could read data that other sites stored in Atlas, including their cookies and sign-ins. Avoid signing in to important accounts, such as email, banking or school accounts, through Atlas.

Permissions

Atlas asks before a site can use your camera, microphone or location, and remembers your choice for that site only. You can change it in the site info panel.

Children

Atlas is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Your choices and contact

You can reject ad cookies, clear Atlas's data, or stop using Atlas at any time. For questions or requests, contact us on our Discord server. We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
